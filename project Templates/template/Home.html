Welcome to Smart Agri-Maintenance System

In the era of modern agriculture, machinery efficiency is crucial for productivity. Our Predictive Maintenance System leverages advanced machine learning algorithms to monitor the health of agricultural equipment in real-time, ensuring timely maintenance and minimizing unexpected failures.

Key Features

  • User-Friendly Interface: Easily upload equipment data and receive actionable insights.
  • Advanced Machine Learning Models: Includes LSTM, BiLSTM, ANN, and LGBM for highly accurate predictions.
  • Data Visualization: Interactive charts and performance metrics help understand system predictions clearly.
  • Comprehensive Dashboard: Track equipment status, maintenance schedules, and historical trends.
  • Secure and Reliable: Built on Django Framework, ensuring secure user authentication and data management.

Why Choose Our System?

  • Reduce Downtime: Predict failures and schedule maintenance proactively.
  • Optimize Costs: Minimize unnecessary repairs and extend machinery life.
  • Data-Driven Decisions: Make informed decisions using real-time analytics.
  • Easy Access Anywhere: Cloud-based data management for remote monitoring.
